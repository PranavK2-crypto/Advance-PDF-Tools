Frequently Asked Questions

How to upload multiple images or folders?
Click the upload area and select multiple files (Ctrl+Click / Cmd+Click), or drag & drop multiple images. You can also drag an entire folder — the tool will automatically scan and load all images inside.
How to download all resized images at once?
Click the green "Download All Images" button. All your resized images will be saved in the selected format. For PDF format, you can choose between separate PDFs or a single combined PDF.
How to remove an image from the queue?
Click the small ✖ (cross) icon on the top‑right corner of any image thumbnail in the queue to remove it.
Does this upload my images to a server?
Absolutely not! Everything runs locally in your browser. Your images never leave your device. No uploads, no servers, no tracking — 100% private.
